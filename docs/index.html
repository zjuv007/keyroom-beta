What's inside

Four places, one idea: know where the notes are, then make music with them.

Learn

This week's scale, box by box. Move it to other keys, play over backing tracks, and let your mic check you before the next week unlocks.

Explore

Any scale in any key: all five boxes, the whole neck, every chord of the key laid out up the neck, and a progression of the day.

Create

Strum or tap chords and Key Room names them. Build a progression, see what to solo with and which chord fits next, then jam over it.
